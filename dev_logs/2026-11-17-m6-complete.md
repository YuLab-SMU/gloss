## Surprises

- The fixed image repaired material generation but exposed a real host/container layout difference on all six old held-out host materials. → M6 plan scope change; container materials become the future canonical held-out inputs.
- Data-key-only anchors silently moved to another element in 7/25 controlled updates. Multi-selector anchors stayed safe. → M6 plan scope change; data-key-only remains an ablation.
- Two models were operationally affordable for one development repeat, but the result still cannot support a confidence interval or the main A-versus-B claim. → M6 plan scope change; repeated precision and held-out extension remain next-slice work.
- The open-world human audit could not be staffed in this session. → M6 plan scope change; masked scoring is not promoted to the primary open-world metric.
- Engineering baselines had no runnable integration in the selected figures. → M6 plan scope change; only availability/integration reasons are reported.
