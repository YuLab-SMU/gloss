## What changed

Added the M5 bench layer under `bench/agent/` and `bench/semantic/`.

- `m5-material.R` writes source-hashed raw, bundle and prepared-view materials for the registered development and held-out subsets. The pinned image lacked `svglite`, so the selected host-material figures were generated with the same `gloss` capture and export path on the host.
- `m5-pilot.R` builds directional trend tasks and runs A, A + check, B − check and B through one fixed loop. `score-m5.R` scores archived traces without model calls.
- `m5_checks.R` measures true and false claims, transformed data, markup rejection, masked binding, updates, selector ablation and SVG preflight.
- The plan, predictions, design notes and archives record the cost gate and the resulting single-model held-out scope.

## Surprises

- The pinned bench image has no `svglite`, so `GLOSS_BENCH_OFFLINE=true bench/docker/run.sh Rscript bench/agent/m5-material.R ...` stops at package loading. The image defect is routed to the M5 plan scope change. Host material generation succeeded for six selected figures per set.
- Gemini latency is much higher than GLM latency. The first A run used 135.7 seconds, which made the registered two-model five-repeat matrix too expensive to complete in this slice. The held-out scope is narrowed to one GLM and one repeat, and the unrun model claim remains open.
- The B − check condition produced incorrect or unsupported verdicts on three of four held-out tasks while B check stayed correct on all four. This supports the checking mechanism on the registered slice and does not establish a general A versus B accuracy gain.
- Two selected held-out materials had no numeric directional task. They remain in the material archive and are not assigned invented labels. → plan Scope changes.
